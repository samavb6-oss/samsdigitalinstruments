About this site

Sam's Digital Instruments is a small, independent collection of everyday tools — a flip clock with a live world-clock row, a calculator with a scientific mode, and a unit, weight, temperature and currency converter — built to be fast, clear, and free of clutter.

Simplicity

The site does three things and doesn't try to do more. No dashboards, no settings pages, no onboarding flow — you open a tool, use it, and leave. That constraint is intentional: it's what keeps the page fast and keeps the tools easy to reach for.

Usefulness

Every tool here answers a genuinely common, everyday question — what time is it somewhere else, what's this discount worth, what does this measurement convert to. Nothing is included just to pad the site out; if a tool doesn't earn its place, it doesn't go on the page.

Privacy

There's no account system and nothing you type into the calculator or converter is saved or stored by SamsDigits. Your tool inputs remain in your browser tab. SamsDigits uses GoatCounter to understand website traffic and usage, such as which pages are visited and how the site is used. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Accessibility

The site is built with keyboard navigation, visible focus states, and screen-reader-friendly labelling in mind, and works the same way on a phone, a tablet, or a desktop monitor without a separate "mobile version."

Speed

No frameworks to download, no build step, minimal CSS and JavaScript. The goal is a page that loads quickly even on a slow connection, because a tool that's slow to open isn't actually saving anyone time.
