Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This page explains what happens to information when you use this website (the "Site"). This Site is a small set of free tools — a clock, a calculator, and a unit/currency converter — and does not require an account or ask you to submit personal information to use them.

Hosting

This Site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Cloudflare, as the hosting provider, may process standard technical request data (such as IP address and browser type) as part of delivering the page to you, in line with Cloudflare's own privacy policy. This Site itself does not separately access or store that data.

Information we collect

We do not directly collect names, email addresses, or other personal details through any tool on this Site. Values you type into the calculator or converter stay in your browser and are not sent to us.

Cookies

This Site uses GoatCounter, a privacy-focused analytics service, to measure website usage. GoatCounter does not use cookies or persistent identifiers to track visitors.

Advertising (not yet active)

This Site does not currently display any advertising. If it does in the future (for example, through Google AdSense), ads may be served by third-party advertising companies that use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. Before that happens, this section will be updated with specifics, and you'll be able to opt out of personalized advertising via Google's Ads Settings or aboutads.info/choices.

Third-party data used by the tools

The currency converter fetches live exchange rates directly from your browser via a third-party public API (open.er-api.com). The request contains the exchange-rate data needed by the tool; as with any network request, the API provider may also receive standard connection data such as your IP address.

Analytics

This Site uses GoatCounter to understand website traffic and usage and to help improve the Site. GoatCounter provides website usage statistics such as page views, referrers, browser information, and approximate geographic information without using cookies or persistent identifiers to track visitors.

Children's privacy

This Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date at the top.
