Why Sam's Digital Instruments?

Most calculator and converter sites bury the actual tool under ads, pop-ups, and a sign-up wall before you can type in a single number. Sam's Digital Instruments exists as the opposite of that — pick a tool, get your answer, nothing standing in the way.

The site is deliberately small. There's no framework to download and no account to create. A lightweight, privacy-focused analytics service is used to understand overall site traffic. Every calculation happens right in your browser — nothing you type is sent anywhere or stored anywhere.

A world clock, a calculator, and a converter cover a huge share of the small, everyday questions people actually search for — what time is it there, what's 15% of this bill, how many kilometres is that in miles. Each one works the same whether you're on a phone, a tablet, or a full-size monitor.

There's no roadmap trying to turn this into something bigger than it needs to be. If a new tool gets added, it'll be because it's genuinely useful and fits the same idea: open the page, get your answer, move on with your day.
